Question 1:
    Combining different sources isn't too terrible of an idea! Different
    authors write in different styles and across different contexts, so this
    can definitely provide variability to the training data to hopefully
    allow the output to be more fluid and different. There could be several
    downsides to this however. If we are mixing authors from technical and
    fantasy genres, this could train fine but predict pretty poorly. If we're
    talking about some technical concept and then end up spewing out some words
    relating to Narnia or some other fantasy land, these two concepts and
    contexts don't make sense together. This could also backfire if our
    authors tend to write in difference sentence lengths / clarities. A longer
    paper has room to be a little more vague and then narrow down, whereas
    shorter papers need to get to the point faster. If this starts happening
    in the middle of sentences, who knows how this could go.
